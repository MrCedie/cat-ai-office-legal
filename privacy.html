This Privacy Policy explains what information Cat AI Office ("we", "us", "our") uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Where Your Data Is Kept

Cat AI Office runs on your own computer. Your projects, videos, settings and connected-account tokens are stored locally on that computer. We do not run a server that collects or stores your data.

2. Information We Use

  • Content you create: topics, scripts, videos, captions and settings you enter in the app.
  • Connected-account access tokens: issued by TikTok, Facebook or YouTube when you connect an account, and used only to publish content you approve.
  • Basic account information from TikTok (user.info.basic: your display name and avatar), used only to show which account is connected.

3. How We Use TikTok Data

With your permission, Cat AI Office uses the TikTok scopes user.info.basic and video.publish to identify your connected account and to upload and publish videos that you have chosen to post or schedule. We do not read your followers, messages, likes or other videos, and we do not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.

4. Third-Party Services

When you use a feature that needs one, Cat AI Office sends data directly from your computer to that service:

  • TikTok, Meta (Facebook) and Google (YouTube): to upload and publish the videos you choose.
  • AI model providers you configure (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Ollama): to generate text, scripts and other content from your prompts.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers.
  • We do not publish anything without your approval.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can delete videos and projects in the app, remove access tokens from the app's configuration, and revoke Cat AI Office's access at any time in your TikTok, Facebook or Google account settings. You can also email us to ask about or delete any data related to you.

7. Security

Access tokens are kept in a local configuration file that is not shared or uploaded. Keep your computer and accounts secure, as anyone with access to your computer may be able to use the app.

8. Children

Cat AI Office is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date above will change.
